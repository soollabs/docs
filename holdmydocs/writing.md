Everything you write in HMD becomes a Markdown file with a revision history, and you never need Git commands. New here? Start with [[Your First Wiki]].

## Write and revise

- [[Pages]]: create a page, change its path, review history, restore an older version, and what happens when two people edit at once.
- [[Markdown And The Editor]]: format text, insert tables and links, and draw Mermaid diagrams.
- [[Attachments]]: upload images and files, link to them, and see which documents are searchable.

## Make information easy to find

- [[Links And Navigation]]: wiki-links, the page tree, tags, backlinks and search.
- [[Namespaces]]: separate projects or audiences, each with its own homepage, page order and appearance.

## Share your work

- A public namespace lets visitors read pages in your running HMD instance. [[Authentication And Access]] covers who can see and edit what.
- [[Static Export]] makes a standalone documentation website. Review the namespace's pages and attachments first: the copy does not inherit your live wiki's access controls.
