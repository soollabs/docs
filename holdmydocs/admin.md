Choose how your wiki looks and who can use it. Personal preferences and administrator controls are separate: not every signed-in user can change the instance configuration.

- [[Settings And Administration]] — find personal preferences and administrator settings, and understand which changes affect everyone.
- [[Authentication And Access]] — configure local accounts or OIDC sign-in and choose read, write, and settings permissions.
- [[Personal Access Tokens]] — issue credentials for scripts and assistants with only the permissions and namespaces they need.

If you are setting up a new instance, start with [[First-Run Setup]]. To change a namespace's homepage, page order, or visibility, use [[Namespaces]].
